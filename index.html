Still Bonsai — Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 September 2026

Still Bonsai does not collect your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party services of any kind. The app makes no network connections. Everything it creates stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your trees. Each sitting is saved as a few numbers — a seed, a style name, a date, a duration and a stillness value — in the app's own private storage. That is what a tree is regrown from. It never leaves the device.
  • Your settings. Preferences such as session length and whether the microphone is enabled are stored on the device.

Heart rate (Apple Health)

If you allow it, Still Bonsai reads recent heart-rate samples from Apple Health so that a calmer sitting can grow a more refined tree. This is optional and the app works fully without it.

  • Health data is read only. The app never writes anything to Apple Health.
  • Readings are held in memory during a sitting and are not saved to a file, not included in an export, and not transmitted anywhere.
  • No health data is ever used for advertising, marketing, profiling or any other purpose.
  • You can withdraw access at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices.

Microphone

If you turn it on, Still Bonsai listens for the rhythm of your breathing so the tree can grow with it. This is off by default.

  • Audio is processed live on the device to estimate a breathing rate. It is never recorded, never saved and never transmitted.
  • No audio ever leaves your device, and nothing is retained after a sitting ends.
  • You can withdraw access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.

Purchases

Still Bonsai Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The app never sees or stores your payment details. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Images you export

Exported images are created on your device and handed to the standard iOS share sheet. Where they go from there is entirely your choice; the app does not upload them anywhere.

Children

Still Bonsai does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored, including every saved tree. There is no copy anywhere else, because nothing was ever sent anywhere else.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
